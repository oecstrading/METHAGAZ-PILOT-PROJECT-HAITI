03 · Scope of Work

Describe in detail the services you will provide to support the entrepreneur’s prototype development. Be specific about deliverables, format, and what the entrepreneur will receive at the end of your engagement.

Type of service Total technology solution provider — dMRV platform integration, mobile application development, IoT data architecture, and digital system commissioning for the METHAGAZ bottled-biogas prototype.
Description of services

The OECS Trading & Investment Company LLC will deliver the digital and data infrastructure required to operate and verify the METHAGAZ prototype: a biogas purification and cylinder-filling station serving low-income households and food vendors in Les Cayes. The engagement does not include the procurement or physical installation of tanks, sensors, cylinders, or filling-station hardware; those items are sourced from third-party suppliers. The OECS Trading & Investment Company LLC’s scope is the software, data, and integration layer that connects field hardware to the GCFT dMRV backbone and the user-facing applications.

Services include

  1. GCFT platform integration for METHAGAZ — Configuration of the GCFT dMRV backbone to ingest, validate, and ledger data from the METHAGAZ filling station and cylinder fleet. Includes API development for hardware-agnostic data ingestion (MQTT/REST), SHA-256 audit-trail configuration for cylinder fill/refill events, and carbon-accounting logic aligned with Gold Standard AMM v2.0 (methane avoidance + charcoal displacement pathways).
  2. Cylinder-fleet and station monitoring dashboard — A web-based operational dashboard for COSMOS SOLUTION to track cylinder fill levels, station throughput, safety parameters (pressure, leak detection), and tester-level consumption. The dashboard generates audit-ready reports for verification bodies and carbon registries.
  3. Mobile applications (iOS + Android) — Two user-facing interfaces:
    • Household / vendor app: refill ordering, delivery tracking, cylinder-deposit management, and consumption history.
    • Station-operator app: filling-station workflow support, safety-checklist logging, and cylinder status updates.
  4. SMS/WhatsApp ordering and notification layer — Integration with Twilio or Meta Cloud API to enable low-bandwidth refill ordering, delivery confirmation, and safety alerts for testers without smartphone access.
  5. Data security, offline resilience, and interoperability — TLS 1.3 in transit and AES-256 at rest; local buffering logic to survive Haiti’s intermittent connectivity; JSON schema documentation so sensor data from third-party hardware flows correctly into GCFT.
  6. Technical documentation and handover — API documentation, operational runbooks, data schema definitions, and a commissioning report confirming that the digital platform is live and audit-ready.
Key deliverable(s)
  • GCFT–METHAGAZ integration live — API bridge operational; cylinder fill/refill events recorded with SHA-256 audit trail; carbon-calculation engine configured for METHAGAZ’s two emission-reduction pathways.
  • Station operations dashboard — Deployed and tested with at least one filling-station operator; dashboard reflects live or simulated station data and produces a sample audit report.
  • Mobile apps on TestFlight and Google Play — household/vendor app functional for ordering and tracking; station-operator app functional for fill logging and safety checks.
  • SMS/WhatsApp ordering layer operational — end-to-end refill request → distributor notification → delivery confirmation tested with pilot users.
  • Source code and documentation handover — app codebase, GCFT integration documentation, data schema, and operational runbooks delivered to COSMOS SOLUTION and Bridge for Billions.
  • Digital commissioning report — confirmation that the platform is stable, data flows are verified, and the dMRV audit trail is ready for third-party review.

Annex C — Cylinder Fill / Refill Operational Workflow
WORKFLOW

End-to-end digital workflow for a single refill cycle, showing where the OECS Trading & Investment Company LLC platform intervenes.

1
Order placed. Household or vendor orders a refill via the mobile app or SMS/WhatsApp. The platform records customer ID, cylinder ID, and delivery address.
2
Order queued & routed. The operations dashboard queues the order and routes it to the filling station. Station operator receives a fill instruction in the station-operator app.
3
Safe fill. Operator fills the cylinder. Third-party pressure/flow/weight sensors capture the fill event. The station-operator app logs the safety checklist. The IoT gateway transmits the fill record to GCFT.
4
Ledger & carbon calculation. GCFT validates the fill record, appends a SHA-256 audit entry, and calculates the emission reduction (methane avoided + charcoal displaced) under AMM v2.0 logic.
5
Delivery & confirmation. Cylinder is delivered. Customer confirms receipt in the app or via SMS. Platform updates cylinder status and consumption history.
6
Audit & reporting. Dashboard generates an audit-ready report for verifiers and carbon registries. Operations team reviews throughput, safety, and consumption analytics.
